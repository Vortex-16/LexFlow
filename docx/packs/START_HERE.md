## Project working name

**LexFlow — Legal Information & Document Navigator**

The name is only a working label. The implementation should remain modular so it can be renamed later.

## Product promise

Help a non-lawyer understand legal information and uploaded legal documents in plain language, compare documents or clauses, locate relevant sections, identify missing context, and find safer next steps — with visible sources, jurisdiction, uncertainty, and escalation guidance.

## Design direction

- Minimal, editorial, professional.
- Black/white/neutral palette with one restrained accent.
- No neon.
- No gratuitous gradients.
- No glassmorphism-heavy UI.
- No decorative dashboards that do not help the task.
- Responsive from mobile to desktop.
- Strong typography, spacing, hierarchy, keyboard support, and readable evidence panels.
